Admission test results are published per university or per cluster — there's no single national portal. Below are the highest-traffic official systems; always confirm you're on the right unit/university before checking a roll number.

GST Admission (Cluster)

Official

Integrated admission system covering ~20 public universities (General, Science & Technology cluster) — one application, one exam per unit, results published here.

gstadmission.ac.bd

nu.ac.bd

06

No internet? Use SMS

এসএমএসের মাধ্যমে ফলাফল

SSC / HSC & equivalents

Format: BOARD-FIRST-3-LETTERS ROLL YEAR, sent to 16222.

SSC DHA 123456 2026
send to 16222

Replace SSC with HSC, DAKHIL or ALIM as needed, and the board code with yours (e.g. DHA, CHI, RAJ, JES, COM, BAR, SYL, DIN, MYM, MAD, TEC).

Primary (PSC / Ebtedayee)

Format: DPE ROLL YEAR, sent to 16222.

DPE 123456 2026
send to 16222

SMS gives a quick pass/fail and GPA; the website gives the full subject-wise mark sheet.
